# Customer Churn & Retention Analysis | Power BI

Find the live interactive version here: https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/bd5a8f51-01a6-4228-9e70-afc060e8d9a2/b12eb775389e5dbc09d8?experience=power-bi

This Power BI dashboard analyzes customer churn to identify patterns and reasons behind customer loss. It focuses on customer demographics, subscription plans, contract length, and payment delays to help businesses understand customer retention.

## Key Dimensions & Features

**Customer Churn Rate:** Shows the percentage of customers who left the service.

**Customer Demographics:** Analyzes customer churn by Gender and Age Group.

**Subscription Type:** Compares churn across Basic, Standard, and Premium plans.

**Contract Length:** Analyzes churn by Monthly, Quarterly, and Annual contracts.

**Payment Delay Analysis:** Examines the average delay before customers leave the service.

**Financial Impact:** Tracks revenue lost from churned customers and compares it with remaining revenue.
